# Pixel + Prism duration comparison — October 3, 2026

Danny requested 15-, 13-, and 11-second tests combining Pixel Party's colored
perimeter squares with Prism Jackpot's line-burst celebration. These are
catalog items 54, 55, and 56, respectively, in All Tests only.

All three use the exact original 54-photo order and Blue winner 88 from
items 39/41. Source photographs are hash-checked before rendering. The motion
curve is scaled proportionally to land at 12, 10, or 8 seconds. Each then holds
the winning photo for three seconds. Pixel perimeter animation continues;
Prism rays replace the original Pixel corner-bracket celebration and are
clipped outside the winning portrait. Accents settle to blue at the landing.

Disco D is edited from the original lossless master, without changing pitch,
tempo (120 BPM), or gain. The 15/13/11-second edits start on source downbeats
0/2/4 seconds and end at source second 15. Each has a 15 ms opening fade and
250 ms closing fade. The final three seconds of the lossless music edits are
sample-identical, and the original 12-second accent matches every landing.

Outputs are 1080 × 1920, 60 fps, H.264/AAC MP4 with fast start. Verification
checks exact frame counts and durations, complete decoding, copied AAC packets,
unchanged photo order, and the common music finish. See `VERIFICATION.json`.

The scripts are an archive of the local rendering job. They run from
`work/headshot-pixel-prism-duration-2026-10-03` in the MIT 2.009 workspace and
depend on the approved local photo inventory and original lossless audio.
Those source portraits are not newly uploaded as individual files. The
website build uses the finished MP4s, not these scripts. Selects and the
18-second browser generator remain unchanged while Danny compares pacing.
