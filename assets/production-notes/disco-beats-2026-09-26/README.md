# MIT 2.009 — Four original disco-beat tests

All four are 18 seconds at 120 BPM. Each has six bars for the spinning reel,
a musical accent at exactly 12 seconds when the winning portrait settles,
and three bars of celebration. They are instrumental, programmed and synthesized
from scratch, using no external recordings or samples.

A / 34 — Mirrorball: warm octave bass, handclaps, guitar, and disco strings.
B / 35 — Neon Boogie: punchy electronic bass, synth chords, and arpeggios.
C / 36 — Pocket Funk: lightly swung clavinet, cowbell, and a playful bass line.
D / 37 — Space Disco: electric keys, shimmering synths, and a wide winning lift.

The MP4s use the same Neon Circuit video (30) for comparison. Its video stream
is copied unchanged. The beat can later accompany any of the four digital looks.
MP3: easy listening and sharing. WAV: 24-bit, 48 kHz stereo for editing.
All four masters are matched to approximately -16 LUFS.
