# MIT 2.009 — Analog confetti reel studies

Two silent 18-second vertical MP4s, 1080 × 1920 at 60 fps. Both develop catalog version 25: Blue Team photo 88, with neighboring portraits visible, the same 54 mixed-team portraits and order, and a landing complete at 12 seconds.

26. Analog party poppers: two unequal bursts, separated by a fraction of a second, release loose clumps. The pieces peel apart, hang, slip sideways, and fall at different rates. There are 1,560 pieces.

27. Analog paper drift: irregular waves of tossed paper catch shared air currents, with a looser, slower shower. There are 1,500 pieces.

Both feature varied hand-cut silhouettes, uneven edges, folds, paper grain, lighter reverse sides, and pieces that flex as they turn. A smaller set of large, soft pieces passes near the camera. Foreground paper can cross the face naturally. These are simulated paper effects, not footage of real confetti.

The reel has a small hesitation and unequal settling rebounds. Warm light remains fixed inside the housing as portraits pass through it: three unequal pools suggest hidden bulbs, with darker edge recesses. The camera and image framing remain steady.
